Meet LightImage.

1 min 27 sec · Vietnamese narration and subtitles

From the whole photo to the smallest detail.

English and Vietnamese interface

Open LightImage → Settings… with ⌘, or click the gear icon. Choose a language to switch immediately. LightImage remembers your choice the next time you open it.

App settings

See your installed version, check for updates on demand, or toggle automatic checks. Set your default export format, JPEG quality, and dimensions, and choose whether to show exported photos in Finder.

Color temperature

Under White Balance & Color, adjust RAW files in Kelvin, starting from the camera’s white balance. JPEG and TIFF use a relative scale from −100 to +100.

Drag left to cool the image or right to warm it, or enter a value. Use Tint to correct a color cast and Reset to restore the original white balance.

AI object removal

Paint over the object and click Fill with AI. LaMa fills the area based on the surrounding image. The model downloads on first use (about 217 MB), and photos are processed on your Mac.

Lens Blur

Detect the subject, keep it sharp, and adjust background blur with a slider.

Detail and photo restoration

Enhance fine detail and reduce luminance and color noise. For older photos, reduce small spots and restore faded contrast.

Batch editing and export

Select multiple photos and apply a preset to them at once. Export the selection or every edited photo in the library, including photos outside the current filter.

Light and color

Fine-tune exposure, white balance, HSL, RGB curves, and color grading. Adjust grain, vignettes, sharpening, and noise reduction.

Masks and edge-aware brushing

Detect the sky, subject, or background. Paint or erase directly on the mask with a live stroke preview, and undo individual strokes with ⌘Z.

On-device portrait editing

Brighten or smooth skin, remove blemishes, and slim faces individually or for the whole group. Apple Vision analyzes the photo on your Mac.

Compare matching details

View Before and After side by side or use Split View. Scroll to zoom and drag to pan both views together when checking skin tones and fine details.

Library management

Import photos or folders, rate and flag images, create virtual copies, or sync edits. LightImage saves edits per photo and preserves the original files.

Framing and export

Crop, rotate, flip, and straighten. Export JPEG, PNG, or 16-bit TIFF in sRGB at the original resolution or a size you choose.

Try the Before and After slider.

Lightroom compatibility

Bring your existing presets.

Import preset files or entire folders. Adjust the amount for one photo, apply presets at 100% to selected photos, or save your own color adjustments as a preset.

Colors may differ from Lightroom because each app uses its own renderer. Import support for Adobe profiles, lens corrections, and Lightroom masks is limited.

RAW support depends on your camera and the macOS decoder. JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and TIFF files can also be opened.

Install LightImage.

Install on Mac

Check the chip or processor in  → About This Mac, then choose the matching installer.

Open the DMG and drag LightImage to Applications, then open it from there. If you use the ZIP, extract it to get LightImage.app.

The app is ad-hoc signed. If macOS blocks it, see the first-launch instructions.

Build with Xcode

Open Package.swift in Xcode, select the LightImage scheme and My Mac destination, and press ⌘R to run.

Restart to Update

LightImage can check for and download updates in the background. Once the signature is verified, Restart to Update appears in the lower-right corner. Click it to save, install, and reopen when you are ready.

In 26.1.1 or later, open Settings → Updates to check now or toggle automatic checks. You can also choose LightImage → Check for Updates….

If the update button is missing in 26.0.1, 26.0.3, or 26.0.4, install the latest version manually once.

Open source.

A native app built with SwiftUI, AppKit, and Core Image. The repository includes build instructions, sample presets, and tests. Explore the code, report bugs, or suggest improvements on GitHub.
